How to play

Three rules

  1. 01

    Start

    Click inside the game. The first level starts straight away.

  2. 02

    Move

    Steer the heart up and down with W and S, or with the arrow keys.

  3. 03

    Dodge

    Swords fly in from the right. Dodge 25 of them to clear the level. One hit and it's game over, with your best run on the scoreboard.

About the game

Hayu started in 2024 as my way into Unity: a sprite that moves, obstacles that spawn and a score that counts up.

I kept going until it was a finished game, with levels, a city that scrolls past in layers and a proper game-over screen. It's small, but it's done, and it's the first game of mine that anyone can play.
