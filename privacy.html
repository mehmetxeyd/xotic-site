Privacy

Privacy policy

In short: this site sets no cookies, has no analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from third parties until you ask it to. Below is exactly what happens to your data and why.

1. Who is responsible

The controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Mehmet X (XOTIC)
Berlin, Germany

Email: mehmet.xeyd@gmail.com

2. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, your browser sends technical data that Cloudflare needs to deliver it and protect it from attacks. This includes your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the referring page, and your browser and operating system.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Cloudflare is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission considers adequate (Art. 45 GDPR). More information: Cloudflare privacy policy.

3. Cookies, analytics and fonts

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools. All fonts are hosted on this domain, so no connection is made to Google or any other font service.

4. Contact form

If you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message. We use this data only to answer your enquiry.

The form is sent through Web3Forms (privacy policy), which forwards your message to us by email. Web3Forms may process data outside the EU. That is why the form asks for your consent before sending.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and, where your message concerns a possible project, steps taken before entering a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us. We delete your messages once your enquiry is fully dealt with, unless we have to keep them by law (for example, tax records for paid work).

If you prefer, you can simply email mehmet.xeyd@gmail.com instead.

5. Email

If you email us, your message is received through Gmail, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google may also process data in the USA; Google is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We use the content of your email only to reply to you, on the same legal bases as in section 4.

6. GitHub repository list (Work page)

The Work page can show a live list of my public code repositories from GitHub. This list is only loaded when you click the button to load it. Your browser then connects to GitHub (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which receives your IP address and browser details. GitHub is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

The legal basis is your consent, given by clicking the button (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). For the rest of your visit, the list is kept in your browser's session storage so it doesn't need to be fetched again. It is deleted when you close the tab. See the GitHub privacy statement.

7. Hayu, the game on this site

The Hayu page lets you play my game in your browser. The game only loads when you press Play, and it loads entirely from this website. No third-party servers are involved, and nothing about your play is sent to us or anyone else.

While you play, the game keeps its own settings and your best score in your browser's storage on your device (IndexedDB), and the page remembers your volume setting (local storage). This data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Storing it is necessary for the game you chose to play (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

8. Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.), itch.io (Itch Corp., where Hayu is also published), GitHub, a project demo hosted on GitHub Pages, and zen.xotic.dev. No Instagram content is embedded on this site. Those sites only receive data from you if you follow a link. Their own privacy policies then apply.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you (Art. 15)
  • have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17)
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18)
  • receive it in a portable format (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing done before you withdrew it (Art. 7(3))
  • complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77)

To use any of these rights, email mehmet.xeyd@gmail.com.

10. No automated decisions

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.
