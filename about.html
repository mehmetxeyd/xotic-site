About

Two crafts,
one way of seeing.

I'm Mehmet X, and XOTIC is the name I put on my photos. Photography is my passion project. I started with a camera at car meets and classic car days around Berlin, learning to find the frame inside a busy scene: what to include, what to cut, where the eye should land first.

By day I work in cybersecurity, as a junior PKI consultant. It's a different kind of detail work: certificates, keys, and making sure the right things are trusted. That curiosity led to side projects like an on-chain credential revocation prototype on Solana.

Building websites turned out to be the same exercise as photography, with type and grids instead of light and glass. Every layout is a composition, and every page has a frame. I build them for myself and, now and then, for others.

The full list is on my CV, and more photos are on Instagram.
