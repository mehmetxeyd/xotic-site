Hello

I'm Mehmet X. XOTIC is my one-person studio in Berlin, for car photography and websites built by hand.

Most of my photos are taken where cars gather: meets, classic car days, the kerb outside a showroom on a Saturday afternoon. I'm rarely after the whole car. I look for the part people walk past, like a badge, a wing or a line of light along a door.

The rest of the time I build websites. They're small, fast and written by hand, with no page builder underneath. The habits are the same as with the camera: crop hard, keep what matters, and make it load before anyone gets bored.
